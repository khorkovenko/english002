      How to shadow

      1. Listen twice without speaking. Notice which words are stressed and which are squashed (to, for, and, the).
      2. Mumble along on the third play at the same speed, copying the melody only.
      3. Speak with full words, half a second behind the voice. Press Record at the same time so recognition can check you.
      4. Compare with "Model, then me". Listen for one thing at a time: rhythm, then one vowel, then one consonant.
      5. Fix one thing, record again. Two or three takes per sentence, then move on; come back tomorrow.
      6. Once a week keep a recording. In a month you will hear the change.

      The model voice comes from your device's speech engine. On Android, install Google Text-to-speech and pick a "Google" or "Natural" voice in the Voice menu. For a real human model, search the same sentence on YouGlish or shadow one line of a podcast whose transcript you have.

      Speech recognition works in Chrome and Edge. It is a rough check: if it hears a different word, that word is where your sound is off.

      Today: 0 of 5 blocks done. The plan is a cycle: input first (listen, read), then output (shadow, speak, write).

      Listening 15 min

      • One 3–5 minute clip at natural speed, no subtitles. Write down what it was about in two lines.
      • Second listen with the transcript. Mark 5 chunks you understood only when you saw them.
      • Put the 5 chunks into LinguaPilot as cards.

      Shadowing and accent 15 min

      • Two sounds from the Sounds tab: hear, watch the mouth, say the words and the contrast pair.
      • Three sentences in the Shadowing lab, two or three takes each.
      • Then shadow 60 seconds of today's listening clip line by line.

      Reading 15 min

      • One article or chapter section. Read once for meaning without stopping.
      • Second pass: collect 8 chunks (collocations, phrasal verbs, linking phrases), not single words.
      • Close it and write a 3-sentence summary from memory using two of the chunks.

      Speaking 10 min

      • Two-minute monologue on a prompt (opinion, story, explanation). Record it in the Shadowing lab with your own text, or use ChatGPT voice mode.
      • Get a transcript, paste it to ChatGPT with the prompt below.
      • Say the corrected version aloud twice.

      Writing 15 min

      • 150–200 words. Mon opinion, Tue formal email, Wed article summary, Thu story or description, Fri report or proposal.
      • Use at least 3 chunks from today's reading and listening.
      • Paste to ChatGPT with the prompt below. Copy every corrected error into your error log in LinguaPilot.

      Vocabulary through the day

      • Review due cards in LinguaPilot or WordApp in dead moments (queue, bus). Ten new items a day is enough if you keep them all.
      • Every card is a chunk with an example sentence you wrote yourself.
      • Saturday: rewrite one of this week's texts using 10 cards. Sunday: a film or episode with English subtitles, plus 30 minutes of conversation.
